Terms, Refunds and Privacy

Terms of Sale

Dogfile is free to download and use. Dogfile Pro is a one-time purchase of $19.99 (plus any sales tax shown at checkout). Buying Pro gives you a personal license to use the Pro features on up to 2 PCs at a time, with updates included. You can move the license between PCs by deactivating one in Settings › License.

Payments are processed by Stripe. Dogfile never sees or stores your card details.

Dogfile is provided as is. Please keep backups of anything important.

Refunds

If Pro doesn’t work for you, contact us within 30 days of purchase with your serial number or the email you paid with, and we’ll refund you. A refunded serial number stops working.

Privacy

On This Website

  • No analytics, no tracking and no advertising cookies.
  • Your light or dark choice is remembered in your own browser.
  • Messages sent through the contact form are delivered by FormSubmit to our inbox, and used only to answer you.

When You Buy

  • Stripe handles the payment. We receive your email address, the amount, and your serial number, and keep them to deliver and support your license.
  • When you activate Pro or start a trial, Dogfile sends an anonymous ID for that PC so a serial number can be limited to 2 PCs and a trial to one per PC. It doesn’t include your name or your files.

In the App

  • Your files stay on your PC. The AI assistant and its optional language model run locally.
  • Dogfile checks for updates from GitHub. Remote connections, phone transfer and features you install talk to the servers or devices you choose.

Questions about your data, or want it deleted? Contact us.
