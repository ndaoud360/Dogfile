FAQ

Is the free version really free?

Yes. No time limit, no ads, no account.

Can I try Pro first?

Yes, for 14 days, from inside Dogfile. No card needed. When the trial ends, Dogfile keeps working as the free version and your files and settings stay as they were.

How do I activate Pro after buying?

Your serial number is on the page you land on after paying. In Dogfile choose Settings › License › Enter serial number and paste it in. If Dogfile is open when you pay, it activates by itself.

I got a new PC. Can I move my license?

Yes. On the old PC choose Settings › License › Deactivate this PC, then enter your serial number on the new one. If the old PC is gone, contact us and we’ll free the slot.

Does the AI assistant send my files anywhere?

No. It runs on your PC, and so does its optional language model.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 30 days of buying. Contact us with your serial number or the email you paid with.
